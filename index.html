BRAID

Mar 2024 - May 2025

BrAID - Real-Time Text-to-Braille Converter

  • Led a three-member team in developing a glove-based wearable that captured printed text with an ESP32-CAM, recognized it using Google Cloud Vision OCR, converted it through the liblouis Braille engine, and delivered six-dot tactile output through a custom actuator.
  • Integrated embedded hardware, a Bluetooth-linked Flask web interface, multilingual Braille conversion, adjustable output speed, and YOLOv11-based environmental object detection into a portable prototype.
  • Built a 114.5 g prototype at a production cost of $78.03; achieved 0.343-second average OCR inference and YOLOv11 performance of 91.8% mAP@50, 96.7% precision, and 92.9% recall.
  • Received the Minister of Science and ICT Award at Korea Code Fair and represented South Korea in the Embedded Systems category at ISEF.

GLIDECANE

Mar 2025 - Nov 2025

GlideCane - A Smart Cane for Vision-Assisted Navigation

  • Independently designed and built a Raspberry Pi-based smart cane using PiCamera2 and TensorFlow Lite to detect obstacles and estimate their distance and direction in real time.
  • Developed a multi-factor danger-scoring pipeline and one-dimensional Kalman filter that reduced directional-signal variation by approximately 74.7%, translating obstacle direction, severity, and category into dual-motor haptic patterns.
